    02 · Approach

    From a paper sketch to go-live.

    Most of these started as a handwritten flow or a spreadsheet someone was tired of updating by hand.

    01

    Map the real process

    I sit with the team, take the flow as they actually run it, often from a whiteboard or paper sketch, and agree each stage and owner.

    02

    Build on what they have

    Google Sheets stays the database, Apps Script becomes the API, so there's no new infrastructure to buy or maintain.

    03

    Make it fast and strict

    One read on load, logic in the browser, locked writes only when data changes, and role-based screens so each person sees their own queue.

    04

    Roll out and support

    Launch mails, user groups and quick fixes after go-live, then the next version based on how people really use it.
